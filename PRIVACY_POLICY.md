Privacy Policy

Where is the Question? does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

The extension only runs on ChatGPT (chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com), Gemini (gemini.google.com), and Claude (claude.ai) pages and does not access data from other websites.

To show markers for every question in long conversations, the extension may read the open conversation from the same site you are using (for example, chatgpt.com or claude.ai), using your existing login in the browser. This data stays in your browser and is used only to list and locate your questions on the page. It is never sent anywhere else.

The only data the extension stores is your list of favorite questions (question ID, a short text preview, and the conversation page address). It is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API.

No third-party services, analytics tools, or remote code are used.

If you have any questions, please contact the developer.
